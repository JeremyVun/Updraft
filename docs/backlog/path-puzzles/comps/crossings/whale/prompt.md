## The encounter: a whale asleep across the way (the one beat the boat waits for)
The crossing from the little boats to the meadow, afternoon, open sea, the meadow island a grey shape in haze off to
the side as in the captures. A huge whale, about 14 m long, lies asleep at the surface broadside across the boat's way
like a long low island: smooth dark slate-blue back with a paler underside, small eye shut, a few barnacles, simple
rounded stylised forms (not realistic, not cartoon-faced). It breathes slowly; the blowhole is on the near side of its
back. The boat has drifted to a stop alongside it, about 6 to 8 m off its flank. The whale is the subject: large in
frame, but the boat, the child and the cygnet stay readable.

Keyframes:
1. `k1-asleep.jpg`: the sleeping whale across the way, the boat stopped beside it, the sail slack. A gull stands on its
   back, asleep too. The child leans over the gunwale toward it; the cygnet has half ducked into the satchel and peeks
   out over the flap. A faint breath of mist hangs over the blowhole.
2. `k2-tickled.jpg`: the player sweeps a gust along its back (thin white wind lines); its skin shivers in a ripple,
   one long pectoral flipper lifts lazily and slaps the water, throwing a little splash toward the boat; the gull
   hops up. The whale is still asleep.
3. `k3-the-breath.jpg`: the player circles over the blowhole: a faint spiral of thin white wind lines stands over it,
   and the whale draws a breath and spouts a tall plume of spray up through the spiral, a faint rainbow in it where the
   sun catches. Its eye has opened.
4. `k4-goodbye.jpg`: it rolls and sinks beyond the boat, flukes lifted high and dripping as if waving; the swell it
   leaves lifts the boat; the child waves after it; the cygnet is all the way out of the satchel, neck up.
