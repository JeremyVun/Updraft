## The encounter: sailing through a meadow on the sea (optional; the boat sails on whether or not the player plays)
The first crossing, between the restored green island behind and the island of lines ahead (still hidden in haze).
Late afternoon, sun ahead and to the left as in `ref-crossing-45s.png`. Partway across, the sea ahead becomes a meadow:
long green grass, the same soft grass the islands have, growing out of the open sea and standing up in the light,
rolling with the swell like a field in wind. It has no hard edge: open water thins into scattered tufts, then dense
grass, then tufts and water again; glints of water between the blades. The boat sails straight on through it as if it
were water. Afterwards it is open sea again.

Keyframes:
1. `k1-into-the-meadow.jpg`: the boat just entering it. The hull parts the grass like a bow wave; behind it the wake is
   a furrow of bent-over blades already springing back up. The child leans to touch the blades going by; the cygnet's
   head is up out of the satchel, looking.
2. `k2-the-winds-wave.jpg`: the player has swept a gust across the meadow beside the boat (a few thin white wind
   lines along the stroke). A broad bright wave rolls through the grass along the stroke, blades bowing and their
   paler undersides catching the sun, and small meadow flowers (white, pale yellow, a little blue) have opened in the
   track the wind took, a ribbon of flowers across the green.
3. `k3-the-trail.jpg`: further on, looking ahead with the meadow thinning back to open sea; behind and beside the boat
   several ribbons of flowers the player's gusts left criss-cross the green.
