# The drowned village: concept frames

Painted by Astra over in-game frames (`prompt.md`), on the route agreed in `../../design.md` ("The whole room, from
the beginning"). The plan is drawn over the real layout from main (north is down): A the cat's roof, B where the boat
strands, 1 the tree, 2 the swing, D the nave ridge by the spire, E where the boat comes alongside; the arrows are the
dark coming from behind.

The paintings set composition, readability and mood, not detail: the game's houses stay simple soft shapes, and the
ivy, railings and flotsam are a few pieces placed where they help the eye. Two frames (`2-drawn-back`, `portrait`)
look down from above roof height; in the game the lens may rise to roof height for the run but never surveys the
village from above. The drawn-back village in `2-drawn-back` shows more than the metre or two the design asks for;
how far the water falls is for the build to tune by eye.
