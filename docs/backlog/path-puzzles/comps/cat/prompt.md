You are the character designer for Updraft, a wordless, dreamy Ghibli-soft browser game. Design the model sheet for a small tabby cat that a 3D artist will build in Three.js from soft rounded primitives (ellipsoids and capsules blended together, smooth-shaded, like the game's other animals and the little girl in game-girl-and-boat.png). It must be unmistakably, irresistibly cute in the way the most-loved cat pictures are: a kitten-ish young cat, sweet and alert, never vacant.

Attached:
- current-cat-closeup.png and current-cat-poses.png: the current in-game cat. The character: a small soft tabby, warm grey-brown mackerel stripes, a little M on the brow, white bib, white chin and muzzle, white socks, a thick tail with soft rings. A little bedraggled from a flood: a few damp clumped tufts on the crown and cheeks, never ugly.

Cute-cat construction to follow: a big round head relative to the body; eyes large but with big dark pupils (dilated, soft) and a clear catchlight, set at or a little below the middle of the head and fairly close together, with soft upper lids so the look is gentle, not staring; a short muzzle, the small pink nose and the mouth tucked close under the eyes; round cheeks; ears set wide and slightly forward. No visible neck when it sits: the head sits down into the shoulders. The sit is a compact pear: wide haunches, front legs straight and together, front paws side by side, tail wrapped round the front paws. Frightened means low: body pressed down, ears flat to the sides, tail tucked or wrapped tight, pupils wide.

Paint one model sheet image, 16:9, plain soft neutral background, the same cat in every view, clean readable shapes, soft game-like 3D rendering, no text except small view labels:

3. Running (a soft gallop) side view, and walking along a narrow rail side view.
4. Head close-up front and side, with faint construction guides showing where the eyes, nose, mouth and ears sit on the head and the head's size against the body.
5. A small scale strip: the cat sitting beside the girl's boots.

Save it as cat-model-sheet.png in the current working directory.
