# Child front concept v1

Proposed character direction, not approved for implementation. Generated with the built-in image tool from Steam capsule v6 as clothing and rear-silhouette reference.

## Jeremy's brief

> great, now as concept art to guide a new child model uplift, how do you think the front of the child should look like?

## Design intent

An ordinary small child lost in a dream, learning courage by caring for a baby swan. Quiet curiosity, a relaxed mouth, rounded cheeks, an oversized soft hood, familiar mustard coat, knitted red scarf and open brown backpack. Preserve simple shapes that read at gameplay distance. No bell or long pointed hood.

The sheet explores a front, three-quarter and close face view. It introduces brown fringe, a tiny nose and brows; those additions need Jeremy's verdict. The generated eyes have more white and the hair more strand detail than intended: for a game model, simplify the hair into a few broad locks and consider smaller, darker eyes. Translate the broad coat folds and scarf knit selectively rather than treating all illustration microtexture as geometry. Preserve the existing companion sockets and validate bird-carry poses if implemented. No game code changed.

## Prompt

Create a carefully designed character concept sheet for a proposed uplift of the child in the game Updraft. Reference image is APPROVED BACK VIEW / clothing/material reference only; develop the previously unseen FRONT. This is the same small child, not a new costume. Landscape sheet on warm off-white plain studio background, no landscape, no text or title, restrained contact shadows. THREE clear views in a single coherent sheet: left a full-body straight front standing neutral view; middle a full-body front three-quarter standing view with the cygnet riding in the open backpack and peeking past shoulder; right a larger head-and-upper-chest close-up in three-quarter view, quietly attentive. Same exact child and clothing construction in every view. Space the views generously, do not overlap. Intended as actionable stylized 3D game modelling reference, softly sculpted forms with clean edges and clear readable construction, NOT photorealism, NOT a busy illustration.

Character intent: an ordinary innocent young child about five or six, lost in a dream, growing brave through caring for a baby swan while trying to find home. Warmth, curiosity and vulnerability, not a fantasy adventurer, jester, doll, mascot or superhero. Gender-neutral appearance. Stylized small dark eyes with restrained tiny catchlights, no large glossy anime eyes, no eyelashes, no huge whites; subtle small brows capable of expression. Rounded warm cheeks without painted circular blush patches, tiny sculpted button nose, small relaxed closed mouth without fixed smile, soft jaw. Short subtly uneven brown fringe, a few broad sculpted locks partially concealed by hood, no intricate hair strands. Eyes and hair stay graphically simple enough for distant game cameras. No tears or sad pout. Their care should emerge from head angle and relaxed posture.

Keep the approved warm mustard yellow oversized A-line coat, hem around the knees, small shoulders and short limbs, slightly large hood framing the face with a soft thick cloth edge and shadow within. Hood is rounded, only a few broad relaxed cloth folds, absolutely NO long point, bell, ornament or pompom. Head including hood about one-third of standing height, youthful but not baby/chibi proportions. Simple coat front placket with 3 small brown buttons, no fancy trim, no unnecessary pockets or extra decorative gear. Long narrow deep brick-red knitted scarf wraps simply around neck without covering mouth; subtle convincing knit visible on close view, two unequal ends trailing quietly to the side, not a cape. Simple warm mustard mittens matching coat (no complex fingers); short dark trousers visible between hem and small dark brown rounded wellington boots. Plain brown soft leather backpack with two shoulder straps visible on front, broad open oval mouth and rounded sagging pouch, NO closed flap. In middle view, fluffy pale grey baby swan comfortably supported INSIDE bag with head and gently curved neck visible past shoulder, small dark beak and dark eye. Bird is not sitting on top of a closed bag. Materials faithful to reference: soft matte coat with restrained folds, textured knitted scarf, smooth worn leather. Neutral warm studio key light, cool soft fill, no dramatic bloom, no texture noise. Every view should have the same face, hood, scarf knot, coat buttons and proportions. Prioritise quiet character and cohesive simple shapes over generic high-detail animated-movie cuteness.
