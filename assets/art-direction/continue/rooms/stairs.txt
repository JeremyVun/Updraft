ROOM: Cloud stairs (the brightest room).
High above the sea in a sky of soft lilac, rose and peach clouds, a cluster of free-floating wooden staircases rises and branches upward: warm brown wood, turned balusters and round newel posts, a deep red carpet runner on each flight, each flight hanging in the air at a different angle. They begin from a small grassy ledge. Low warm sun from the lower left lights their sides; the clouds are painterly, flat-shaded in broad soft shapes, as in the capture. 35 mm lens, looking up at the stairs.
Lower band: a broad cloud bank below the ledge in the shadow of the great cloud above: soft rounded cloud tops in focus, a deep dusky violet and plum, about a third of full brightness, darkening smoothly toward the bottom, no bright rims, no stairs or rails in it; the lowest flight rises from just above it, toward the centre.
