ROOM: Little boats (golden haze).
A soft green island of dense chunky stylised grass curls round a small calm lagoon of pale blue water; on the lagoon float a few tiny white toy sailboats, one with a little red hull. Beyond, through a gap in the grass, the open sea and a bright hazy gold sky, the low sun behind the island to the upper left filling the air with warm light; the far grass glows gold at its tips. 35 mm lens, camera standing in the grass above the lagoon looking down and across it.
Lower band: the grass of the near bank in the island's soft shade: in focus, an even mid-dark green, calm and plain, deepening gently toward the bottom, the bottom-right corner plain shaded grass.
