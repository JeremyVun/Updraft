ROOM: Birches (gold autumn).
A grove of slim pale birch trunks with grey-white bark and dark marks, their crowns a dense mass of small round gold leaves, on a gentle slope carpeted in fallen gold leaves. A long red knitted woollen scarf is strung between the trunks across the middle of the frame in loops and swags, gathered in one knot near the centre of the frame, and trails down the slope through the leaves; a soft pile of gold leaves lies beside a fallen grey branch. The low sun is behind the grove and to the left, a pale lilac-gold sky glimpsed between the trunks, the crowns lit from behind and glowing. 35 mm lens, camera low on the slope, looking slightly uphill into the grove.
Lower band: the grove's long shadow falls across the near slope: a smooth carpet of fallen leaves in even shade, in focus, dim warm russet and umber with a little cool violet, no sun patches, no glowing leaves, darkening smoothly toward the bottom. The scarf's trail ends above it and stays out of the bottom-right corner.
