ROOM: Dark wood (night).
A dark wood at night with no moon and no sky glow. In the centre of the frame floats the waiting coal: a small glowing ember drawn as a soft spiral swirl of orange, rose and cream light, a few tiny sparks drifting up from it and a scatter of tiny orange sparks lying on the forest floor to its right as in the capture. It is the ONLY light in the scene. Its warm light falls off quickly: it lights the thin bare sapling leaning in on its right, the nearest bare twigs and a few fallen leaves around it in warm orange-brown, and nothing much beyond about two metres. Everything else is near-black: the tall trunks and branches are only just discernible as very dark brown-black shapes against a sky that is barely lighter, a deep blue-black, with no stars, no moonlight, no blue rim light and no mist glow. Overall the frame is as dark as the capture: most of it within a few percent of black, a very deep navy-black rather than pure black. 35 mm lens, camera at a child's eye height.
Lower band: the forest floor nearest the lens, near-black, plain, with no sparks or bright leaves in it, and the bottom-right corner plain darkness.
