ROOM: Washing, the island of lines (bright day).
The camera stands low in the long grass on the side of a rounded green hill, a 35 mm lens at a child's eye height, looking a little up the slope. Across the crest of the hill run many thin grey washing lines on plain thin metal posts, sagging gently, hung with pegged white and cream sheets, pillowcases and a few pale yellow and faded-red-hemmed cloths, all lifting softly in a breeze from the left. One large cream sheet with a thin red hem hangs nearest the camera, a little left of centre, its cloth glowing as the low late-afternoon sun shines through it from behind and to the left. The sky is pale warm gold and lilac with soft cloud. The grass is the chunky stylised grass of the capture, bending in a travelling wave.
Lower band: the chunky blades of the slope become steadily shorter and calmer toward the lens over the whole lower third and pass into the soft shade of the hill's curve, so the lowest quarter is a calm, fine, even grass in shade, a mid-dark green, with no edge, stripe or line where the grass changes.
