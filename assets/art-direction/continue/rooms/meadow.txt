ROOM: Meadow (the piano in the field, warm afternoon).
An old upright piano of warm pale wood, its top lid propped open and its keys showing, with a small plain piano stool beside it, stands alone in a field of tall chunky stylised grass on a gentle rise, rolling green and gold pasture fields behind it fading into warm haze. The low sun is high-left and behind, a big soft glow in a pale cream-gold sky, the grass tips lit and glowing; a few white petals lift on the breeze near the piano. 35 mm lens, camera low in the grass, the piano a little left of centre.
Lower band: the chunky blades around the piano become steadily shorter, smaller and calmer toward the lens over the whole lower third, so the lowest quarter is a calm, fine, even grass in the soft shade of the rise, a mid-dark green, with no edge, line or visible point where the grass changes.
