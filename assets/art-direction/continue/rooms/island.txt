ROOM: The still island, before the first gust (grey, the first room of the game).
This island is still and drained of colour: the whole hillside of dense chunky grass is grey, the sky a flat pale grey-white, the sea glassy pale grey-teal and perfectly calm, the sand pale grey. Above on the hill a paper kite (a ruled-paper diamond with a faded red foot and a bow tail) is caught in a rounded grey rock at the crest. A little clinker-built wooden boat lies beached on the wet sand at the water's edge, as the capture shows: run up out of the water, resting on its round bilge and heeled over towards the camera so that we look a little down into it, its bow with the thick white post and small brass lantern pointing to the right along the shore, the stout mast leaning with the hull and the plain cream sail hanging slack. The boat's warm wood and the kite's faded red are the only colour in the frame. Soft, diffuse, shadowless overcast light, a little mist. 35 mm lens, camera on the beach a few metres back, looking along the shore.
Lower band: the darker wet sand and glassy shallow water of the near shore, in focus, plain, still grey (never blue), a smooth mid-grey that deepens gently toward the bottom.
