ROOM: Open sea (sunset).
The sail is NOT plain cloth. In the game, from the birches on, the sail is made of the child's long red knitted scarf: a deep crimson to brick-red wool, matte, a little sun-faded, knitted so that soft vertical ribs of knit stitches run down it and fine horizontal knitted courses cross it, with a slightly lumpy, hand-made edge and soft folds; it reads as a big knitted woollen scarf stretched on the mast and boom, never as smooth sailcloth, satin or nylon. Its shape is the shape in the capture.
A little clinker-built wooden boat with its knitted wool sail sails away from the camera across a wide calm open sea at sunset, leaving a narrow glittering wake. The sky is broad painterly flat-shaded clouds in peach, rose, gold and lilac; the sea mirrors it in rose and violet, with a far tiny smudge of island on the horizon. 35 mm lens, camera a little above and behind the boat. The boat sits in the centre of the frame, slightly below the middle, large enough that the knitted texture of the sail reads.
Lower band: the sea nearest the lens is calm violet-blue water with the sky's rose still faintly in it, in focus, smooth, no glitter; the wake fades out before it and does not run into the bottom-right corner.
