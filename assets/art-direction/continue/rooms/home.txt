ROOM: Home (dusk).
The sail is NOT plain cloth. In the game, from the birches on, the sail is made of the child's long red knitted scarf: a deep crimson to brick-red wool, matte, a little sun-faded, knitted so that soft vertical ribs of knit stitches run down it and fine horizontal knitted courses cross it, with a slightly lumpy, hand-made edge and soft folds; it reads as a big knitted woollen scarf stretched on the mast and boom, never as smooth sailcloth, satin or nylon. Its shape is the shape in the capture.
A small wooden jetty runs out from a pale sandy beach into a calm blue sea. Moored at the end of the jetty is a little round clinker-built boat of warm varnished wood with a cream rubbing strake, a short stout dark mast and boom carrying the knitted wool sail, a small lantern on a thick white post at the bow just lit. Behind, a steep grassy headland of chunky green grass rises to the right with one broad round tree on its slope. The sun is low over the sea behind the headland on the left, a big soft glowing disc in a warm hazy cream-and-peach sky turning toward dusk, its light glittering on the water. 35 mm lens, camera low over the water looking toward the jetty.
Lower band: the sea nearest the lens is deep, calm blue-teal with only faint broad ripples and no glitter, darkening gently toward the bottom-right.
