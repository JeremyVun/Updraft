ROOM: Sky mirror (sunset, a perfectly still sea reflecting the sky).
The sail is NOT plain cloth. In the game, from the birches on, the sail is made of the child's long red knitted scarf: a deep crimson to brick-red wool, matte, a little sun-faded, knitted so that soft vertical ribs of knit stitches run down it and fine horizontal knitted courses cross it, with a slightly lumpy, hand-made edge and soft folds; it reads as a big knitted woollen scarf stretched on the mast and boom, never as smooth sailcloth, satin or nylon. Its shape is the shape in the capture. Here the sail is furled loosely along its boom, its knitted ribs running down the folds.
A short wooden jetty floats on a sea so still it is a perfect mirror of the sky: big painterly flat-shaded clouds in rose, peach and lilac above and the same clouds below. A little clinker-built wooden boat with its knitted wool sail is moored at the jetty, a small lit brass lantern on a thick white post at its bow. Far off a tiny second lantern floats on the mirror. 35 mm lens, camera a little above the jetty looking along it toward the horizon, the jetty and boat in the centre of the frame.
Lower band: the mirror nearest the lens shows the shaded underside of a cloud, a smooth dusky violet-blue with nothing in it, darkening gently toward the bottom-right corner. The jetty ends above it.
