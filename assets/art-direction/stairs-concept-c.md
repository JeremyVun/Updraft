should feel like the stairs that a child would dream about." A soft toy staircase: chunky rounded treads and

and honey wood, a muted dusty-rose runner, undersides dissolving into mist, the top flights going up into cumulus
lit gold at its edges, a gold ghost outline where the missing flight goes. Generated with Astra (`codex exec`) from
a screenshot of the first pass; prompt text in `docs/stairs.md` context and the session that made it.
